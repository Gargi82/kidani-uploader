KidAni Uploader

KidAni Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel Baby Dino World to upload the channel's own finished videos.

It runs on the channel owner's computer. It uploads a video file together with its title, description, tags, "made for kids" setting and scheduled publish time, and optionally sets the video's thumbnail. It does not read, change or delete any other YouTube data, and it is not offered to other users.

It uses the YouTube API Services. Privacy policy
